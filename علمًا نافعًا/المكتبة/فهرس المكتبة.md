## Reading Workflow

Every book should have one primary note using the approved book-note template.

### Required content layers

1. **Back-cover Summary** — publisher or back-cover text with its source.
2. **Quick Summary** — a short personal summary in 3–7 sentences.
3. **Central Ideas** — explanations of the main ideas with chapter or page references when available.
4. **Important Quotes** — short quotations with page or chapter references and a note explaining why each matters.

### Book statuses

- `Planned` — intended for future reading.
- `Reading` — currently being read.
- `Completed` — read and summarized.
- `Paused` — temporarily paused.
- `Abandoned` — stopped with a reason recorded.

New book notes should use the approved template at `AI_Harness/templates/LIBRARY_BOOK_NOTE_TEMPLATE.md`. Existing notes should be migrated in small reviewed batches without overwriting their original content.
