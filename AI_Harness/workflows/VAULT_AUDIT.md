# Vault Audit Workflow

Purpose: inspect the Vault without changing existing knowledge.

1. Confirm the target is the existing Vault root and identify `.obsidian`.
2. Inventory folders and files read-only.
3. Separate `AI_Harness/` from existing knowledge areas.
4. Preserve Arabic names exactly in notes and reports.
5. Identify conflicts, unexpected files, and ambiguous structure.
6. Do not edit, move, rename, delete, migrate, or integrate anything.
7. Record actual scope, findings, and validation in `logs/TASK_LOG.md`.
8. Report limitations and request approval for any follow-up change.

Audit output is descriptive only. It is not authorization to reorganize the Vault.
