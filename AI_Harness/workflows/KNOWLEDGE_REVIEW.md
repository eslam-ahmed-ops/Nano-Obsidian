# Knowledge Review Workflow

Purpose: evaluate a draft before it becomes part of the knowledge base.

1. Locate the draft in `AI_Harness/review/` and verify its source references.
2. Check factual accuracy, duplication, scope, structure, and clarity.
3. Check that no existing note or Arabic folder was changed as a side effect.
4. Identify unsupported claims and items requiring human judgment.
5. Record reviewer decisions and requested revisions.
6. Keep the draft in review until a human explicitly approves integration.
7. If approved, confirm the exact destination and requested change before writing.
8. Record the verified outcome in `logs/TASK_LOG.md` and, for Harness changes, `logs/CHANGE_LOG.md`.

Review is a human decision point, not an automatic publication step.
