# Skill: Knowledge Note Review

## Purpose

Support human review of an AI-generated draft before integration into the knowledge base.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the draft is in the review queue or an explicitly authorized location.
2. Check source support, accuracy, duplication, clarity, and scope.
3. Check that existing notes and folders remain unchanged.
4. List unresolved questions and required human decisions.
5. Record the review outcome and any requested revisions.
6. Require explicit approval before integration.

## Constraints

This skill does not publish, move, rename, or overwrite knowledge notes. It does not treat a completed review checklist as approval unless a human explicitly grants that approval.
